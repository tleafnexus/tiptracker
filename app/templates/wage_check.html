For each workweek ({{ week_start }}–{{ week_end }}) at each employer, this adds your base pay and all tips (cash + card) and compares the total with hours × the local minimum wage. If an employer pays the lower tipped wage and tips don't make up the difference, New Mexico law says the employer must pay the rest. It also checks the base rate against the local tipped cash minimum (e.g. $7.20/hr inside Albuquerque city limits). Rates by location are in Settings.
